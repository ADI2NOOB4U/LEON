# Commercialization and delivery model

LEON should be sold as a controlled AI productivity and workflow-assistance platform with implementation services—not as an autonomous decision-maker.

## Product packages

### Discovery and design

- workflow and data-classification workshops;
- approved-use-case and prohibited-use-case register;
- integration and hosting assessment;
- pilot success metrics and evaluation dataset;
- target architecture and security work plan.

### Department pilot

- isolated deployment for a defined user group;
- configured local models and approved data sources;
- three to five priority workflows;
- administrator and user training;
- weekly quality, privacy, and safety review;
- end-of-pilot value and readiness report.

### Production platform

- department identity and role integration;
- hardened hosting and network configuration;
- observability, audit, backups, and incident support;
- approved model/integration catalogue;
- release, rollback, and change-management process;
- service-level objectives agreed with the department.

### Managed support and change services

- security patches and dependency updates;
- model evaluation and upgrade support;
- workflow and connector development;
- accessibility and language improvements;
- training refreshers and quarterly service reviews;
- data export, migration, and exit assistance.

## Commercial variables

Price these separately so a buyer can compare proposals transparently:

- one-time discovery and deployment;
- number of users or departments;
- hosting and model runtime;
- integration complexity;
- support coverage and response times;
- training and change management;
- security assessment and remediation;
- optional external-provider usage;
- data migration and exit support.

Avoid making public claims about cost savings, accuracy, or compliance until they are supported by the department's pilot evidence.

## Buyer-facing value proposition

> LEON gives public-sector teams a private, permission-aware AI workbench for routine knowledge work, with human approval, data control, and verifiable execution built into the operating model.

## Responsible sales claims

### Safe to claim when evidenced

- local-first deployment is supported;
- deterministic requests can bypass large-model calls;
- tool actions are permission-gated;
- selected actions use verification before success is reported;
- cloud routing can be disabled or restricted by policy;
- the platform has an evaluation and observability foundation.

### Do not claim without formal evidence

- government security certification;
- legal or regulatory compliance;
- autonomous decision-making;
- zero hallucinations or zero risk;
- guaranteed data sovereignty in every deployment;
- production multi-tenancy, identity, or availability if not deployed and tested.
