# LEON Product Brief

## Product

LEON is a local-first AI workspace for public-sector knowledge work. It combines conversation, memory, voice, vision, task planning, research, and approved computer tools behind a single permission-aware operating layer.

## Who it serves

- government officers and administrative staff;
- public-sector support and service-delivery teams;
- policy, research, and programme-management units;
- departments that need AI assistance without sending all working data to a public cloud.

## Why it is different

Most assistants begin with a model call. LEON begins with intent, privacy, capability, and permission. It selects the smallest suitable route, keeps private work local by default, asks before sensitive actions, and verifies the result.

## Product promise

LEON helps staff complete routine work faster while preserving departmental control over data, models, approvals, and records.

## 90-day pilot deliverables

- isolated department deployment;
- configured local model and approved knowledge sources;
- 3–5 priority workflows;
- user onboarding and operating guide;
- baseline versus pilot productivity report;
- security and privacy findings register;
- production-readiness backlog with owners and dates.

## What LEON does not promise

- perfect or authoritative AI answers;
- autonomous government decisions;
- automatic compliance certification;
- elimination of human review;
- zero-risk integration with external systems.

Every official output remains subject to the department's existing approval and records processes.
